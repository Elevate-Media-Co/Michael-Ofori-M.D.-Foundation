01 / A PURPOSE WORTH PURSUING

Health is
human. Access
should be too.

We want essential healthcare resources and opportunities to be within reach of more people, wherever life begins.

The Michael Ofori M.D. Foundation is guided by a commitment to compassionate, equitable support through medical resources, health education and community-based initiatives. Our aspirations are global, with particular attention to under-resourced rural care settings and future healthcare professionals.
